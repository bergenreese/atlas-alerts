About this program

Atlas is a personal safety-monitoring application that Bergen Reese runs on his own computer. Atlas Alerts is its text-message channel: when Atlas detects that Bergen may need help during a health emergency (for example, a dangerously low blood sugar he may not be able to treat himself), it texts a small, fixed list of his emergency contacts from +1 (303) 647-6496 asking them to check on him. It also sends occasional test alerts so the alert path is known to work.

Recipients are only people Bergen personally chose as emergency contacts (family and close friends) who agreed to receive these alerts. There is no public sign-up, no marketing, no purchased or shared lists, and no messages to anyone else.

How people opt in (consent)

To join, text ALERTS to +1 (303) 647-6496. Bergen invites each person he wants as an emergency contact, in person or by personal message, and shares this number; the person opts in by texting ALERTS (or replying YES to his invitation) from their own phone. They then receive one confirmation:

Atlas Alerts (Bergen Reese): You're now opted in to emergency alerts about Bergen. Msg frequency varies. Msg & data rates may apply. For help, reply HELP. To opt out, reply STOP.

Nobody is added without opting in, and there is no web sign-up form. Current contacts opted in by replying YES to this invitation:

Hey, it is Atlas, Bergen's assistant. Bergen asked me to check with you: is it OK if I text you from 303-647-6496 if he ever has a health emergency (like a bad low)? Reply YES to agree.

Only a person who opts in is enrolled. Anyone who declines or doesn't answer never receives an alert. Consent is not a condition of any purchase.

Privacy policy

Effective October 5, 2026

What we collect. Each contact's name and phone number, the alerts sent to them, delivery status, and the contact's consent or opt-out status. Contacts are added manually after they opt in.

How it's used. Only to send Atlas Alerts (notices that Bergen may need help during a health emergency, and occasional test alerts) and to honor opt-out requests.

Sharing. No mobile information is shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties. Information is processed only by the messaging provider (Twilio) and the recipient's carrier to deliver messages. Data is never sold.

Opting out. Reply STOP at any time to stop receiving messages; you'll get one confirmation and nothing further. Reply START to resume.

Security and retention. Records are kept on Bergen's personal computer and with the messaging provider, protected by reasonable safeguards, and kept only as long as needed.

Contact. Bergen Reese, bergenreese@gmail.com.
